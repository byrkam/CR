Field tips
Country:
Two-letter regulatory country code (e.g. GR, US, DE). Mapped to country_code in hostapd.
AP N:
Logical AP index. Keep the AP 1:, AP 2: lines as they are.
SSID:
Broadcast Wi-Fi network name, e.g. SSID: "lab-open".
Band:
Frequency band for the AP, e.g. 2.4 GHz or 5 GHz.
Channel:
Channel number within the chosen band. Make sure it fits the country.
Security:
Fixed by the preset. Do not change it manually.
Passphrase:
Shared secret for WPA2/WPA3-Personal presets.
Stations:
Number of simulated clients that will connect to this AP.
RADIUS Address / Port:
Enterprise preset settings mapped to hostapd authenticator fields.
Shared secret:
RADIUS shared secret used by hostapd.
