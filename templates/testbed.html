Field tips
Country:
Two-letter regulatory country code (e.g. GR, US, DE). Mapped to country_code in hostapd.
AP N:
Logical access point index. Keep the AP 1:, AP 2: lines as they are; they define separate APs.
SSID:
Network name broadcast by the AP (what clients see in the Wi-Fi list). Use quotes, e.g. SSID: "lab-open".
Band:
Frequency band for this AP, e.g. 2.4 GHz or 5 GHz. This controls hostapd hw_mode and default channel range.
Channel:
Channel number within the band (e.g. 1–13 for 2.4 GHz, 36+ for 5 GHz). Make sure it’s valid in your selected country.
Security:
Fixed by the preset (open, wpa2-psk, wpa3-sae, wpa2-enterprise, wpa3-enterprise). Do not change this line.
Passphrase:
Shared secret for WPA2/WPA3-Personal presets. All STAs will use this in their wpa_supplicant configs.
Stations:
Number of simulated clients that will connect to this AP. Each becomes its own Linux namespace + interface.
RADIUS Address / Port:
IP and UDP port of your RADIUS server for Enterprise presets. Mapped to auth_server_addr and auth_server_port.
Shared secret:
RADIUS shared secret between AP and RADIUS. Used in hostapd auth_server_shared_secret.
